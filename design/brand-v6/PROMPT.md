## Промпт

Use case: logo-brand.
Create a new, exceptionally beautiful illustrated brand logo for the Russian pie delivery brand «ПИРОГОРСК». The name means PIE CITY. The essential idea must be immediately literal and unmistakable: a charming little Baltic town BUILT ENTIRELY OUT OF PIES AND BAKED PASTRY, not an abstract monogram, not an ordinary city with a pie placed beside it.

Subject: a compact, cohesive town of four or five delightful buildings clustered into one clear silhouette. Warm golden pie crust walls, elegant Baltic gabled roofs fashioned from crimped pastry, one friendly taller central tower with a pastry roof, and a large beautiful round pie forming the lower foundation of the town with a few broad hand-crimped edges. A few elegant large pastry slits can double as windows. Hint at the Baltic coast with ONE simple dark-blue wave below the pie town. Recognizably a delicious PIE town rather than gingerbread, candy or a castle. No church spire, no onion dome. Inventive, sophisticated, inviting, generous.

Style: premium illustrated bakery identity, confident hand-drawn contours, exceptionally tasteful editorial craftsmanship, vector-friendly shapes with a little elegant engraved detail. Rich appetite-appealing toasted golden pastry accented with deep ink navy #17263B, a few warm cream highlights. Mostly flat color, restrained tonal shading. No muddy brown palette. No photorealistic food scene, no 3D plastic mascot, no generic stock bakery seal. Make the city concept vivid and generous, while keeping the major shapes legible at website-header size. Strong visual hierarchy, beautiful negative space.

Composition: a finished single horizontal logo on a genuinely transparent background. On the LEFT, the pie-town emblem, compact and carefully composed. On the RIGHT, a large beautifully designed Russian serif wordmark, in deep ink navy, exact text: «ПИРОГОРСК» (letters П И Р О Г О Р С К). The wordmark should feel warm, assured, premium, highly legible, with subtle custom letter character and excellent kerning. Emblem and wordmark have equal optical weight, with enough breathing room. The logo fills the canvas with a comfortable margin. No tagline, no subtitle, no mockup, no presentation board, no variant grid, no extra words, no frame, no watermarks. Deliver one resolved logo design.
