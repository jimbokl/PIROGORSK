Use case: ui-mockup
Asset type: high-fidelity art direction reference board for an existing Russian artisan pie website, Пирогорск, Baltic coast.
Primary request: Design a remarkably refined, warm, premium food storefront with the clarity, hierarchy, restraint, precise spacing and accessible controls associated with Apple design. This is a real bakery selection website, not an Apple product clone.
Composition: a polished wide presentation with a large desktop homepage and a narrow mobile homepage side by side, plus compact examples of a product card and bundle card below. Clean flat browser surfaces, no device frames. The design should be feasible in HTML/CSS.
Brand: warm ivory #fbf9f4, deep Baltic navy #123b4a, warm pale oat and sage, a restrained coral accent. Elegant modest serif Russian headlines paired with crisp readable sans-serif interface, harmonious scale. Generous whitespace, only purposeful separators, soft 20-28px corners, very subtle depth. Avoid outlined boxes everywhere.
Hero: understated compact navigation and small brand wordmark Пирогорск. Large editorial heading “Пироги к общему столу”. One tempting golden pie with a cut slice staged on ivory, subtle fine botanical illustration used sparingly at the edge, not a busy wreath. One primary navy button “Выбрать пироги”, small supporting line “Зеленоградск · Светлогорск · Калининград”.
Below: four spacious category tiles rather than seven cramped ones; quiet compact category links for remaining categories. A section “К столу — всё вместе” with three beautifully balanced compact bundle cards, pie composition on a calm oat surface, short names “Ужин на двоих”, “На компанию”, “Сытное и сладкое”, understandable price and modest add button. Product cards with image, concise title, weight selector, price and add action aligned elegantly.
Mobile: true 390px composition, natural horizontal category browsing with visible next tile, no tiny text, hero typography scaled sensibly, wide comfortable touch controls, compact vertically stacked product content.
Constraints: Original visual concept, no copied Apple interface or logos. Preserve the charming illustrated pie character while making the food central. Prices are preliminary. No fake reviews, no fabricated promotion, no sales urgency, no luxury gold, no dark theme, no glassmorphism, no excessive pill badges, no giant empty cards. Sophisticated food editorial meets exceptionally usable commerce.
